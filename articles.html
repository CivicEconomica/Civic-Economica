Transport, Inequality and the Cost of Going Green

Transport policy is rarely discussed as a question of inequality. Yet in the UK, lower-income households consistently spend a larger share of their income on transport than wealthier households, sometimes twice as much, particularly where car ownership or long commutes are unavoidable. As councils push to decarbonise transport systems, the risk is that policies designed to cut emissions end up raising costs for those least able to absorb them.
